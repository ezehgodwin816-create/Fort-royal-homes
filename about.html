01 — Who We Are

More than property. A place to belong.

Since 2006, Fort-Royal Homes has been developing and delivering residential spaces designed around the way people actually want to live.

Based in Abuja, Nigeria, the company brings together property development, real estate sales, land investment and estate management to create carefully considered living environments.

From individual residences to a complete gated community, our approach is centred on quality, thoughtful planning, security and long-term value.
