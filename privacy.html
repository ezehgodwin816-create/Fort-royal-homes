Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Fort-Royal Homes Limited respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains the general way information may be collected, used and protected when you interact with our website, services, enquiries and inspection requests.

01. Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with the website, we may receive information that you voluntarily provide to us.

  • Name and contact information.
  • Email address and telephone number.
  • Property preferences and enquiry details.
  • Inspection requests and preferred appointment details.
  • Messages and other information you choose to send to us.

We may also receive limited technical information associated with website usage, such as browser, device and general website interaction information.

02. How we use information

Information provided through our website may be used to respond to enquiries, arrange property inspections, communicate about properties and services, and provide requested assistance.

We may also use information to improve our website, services and customer experience, and where appropriate to communicate relevant information about Fort-Royal Homes.

03. Inspection requests

When you request an inspection or virtual tour, information such as your name, contact details, selected property, preferred date and other appointment information may be processed so that the request can be managed.

We may contact you using the details supplied in order to confirm, clarify or follow up on your request.

04. Contact forms

Information submitted through contact or enquiry forms may be used by Fort-Royal Homes to understand your request and respond appropriately.

Please avoid submitting highly sensitive personal information through general website enquiry forms unless specifically requested and appropriate.

05. Information sharing

We do not intend to sell personal information submitted through the website.

Information may be shared where reasonably necessary to provide a requested service, operate website functionality, comply with applicable legal obligations, protect rights, prevent misuse or otherwise support legitimate business operations.

06. Data security

We take reasonable measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or loss. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

07. Cookies and technology

The website may use cookies or similar technologies where required for functionality, analytics, security or improving the browsing experience.

Browser settings may allow you to control or restrict certain cookies, although doing so may affect some website functionality.

08. Third-party services

Certain website functions may rely on third-party services or platforms. Such services may process information in accordance with their own policies and terms.

We aim to use reputable service providers appropriate to the website's functions and business requirements.

09. Your choices

You may contact us regarding information you have submitted through the website, including requests to understand, correct or otherwise address your personal information, subject to applicable law and legitimate operational requirements.

10. Policy updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in our website, services, technology or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with an updated date.
